How a project works

Four steps from first message to final files. You know what is being made, and why, before production starts.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We discuss the product, audience, goal and existing material.

    You receive: a clear record of the requirement.

  2. 02

    Brand study

    We review the offer, audience and relevant competitors.

    You receive: the proposed message and approach.

  3. 03

    Written plan

    We agree scope, formats, timeline and review points before production starts.

    You receive: a written plan to approve.

  4. 04

    Production & delivery

    We create the agreed work, review it with you and deliver the agreed files.

    You receive: the final files. Paid-media management is a separate scope.

Questions before you start

Anything else? Ask us directly.

What do you need from us to start?

A short description of the business and what the work should do. Then access to the product or service, any brand files, existing footage or photos, and someone who can answer questions and approve the plan. Anything else is listed in the plan.

What does a video ad or UGC project include?

It depends on the brief. A project can cover scripting, filming or creating the content, editing and versions for each platform. We agree exactly which pieces are included in the written plan before production starts.

Which formats do you deliver?

The ones your platforms need, agreed in the plan. That is commonly vertical 9:16, square 1:1 and wide 16:9 versions, with shorter cut-downs where they are useful.

How are timelines agreed?

We agree the timeline with you after the brief and write it into the plan, with the review points. If anything changes during the project, we tell you early.

Is ad management included?

Not automatically. Making the content and running paid ads are separate scopes. If you want us to plan and manage campaigns, we agree that as part of Marketing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope, so we quote after the first conversation. The quote names each deliverable and format, so you know what you are paying for.
