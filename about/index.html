How we work with you

Every project starts with a conversation about the business and what the work should do.

We then study the offer and the audience, and write down the plan: what we will make, the formats, the timeline and the review points. Production starts once you approve it.

We talk over WhatsApp, email or an online call, whichever suits you. You see the work at the review points agreed in the plan, and we tell you early if anything changes.
