Observe. Understand. Create.

Clear scope. Honest timelines.

Every project follows the same four steps. You always know what we are making, why, and when it lands.

  1. 01

    Talk

    You send a message by the form, WhatsApp or email. We set up a meeting to understand the business, the audience and what you want the work to do.

    From you: a short description and a time for the call.

  2. 02

    Study

    We look closely at the product, the audience, the offer and the competition. This is where the idea comes from.

    From you: access to the product and any brand files you have.

  3. 03

    Agree

    We write down the plan: what we will make, the formats, the price and a date for each deliverable. Nothing starts until you approve it.

    From you: approval of the written plan.

  4. 04

    Make and deliver

    We make the work, review it with you at the points agreed in the plan, and deliver the final files on the agreed date. If anything changes, we tell you early.

    From you: feedback at each review point.

Questions about the process

Do you have fixed packages?

No. We scope every project with you in a meeting, so you only pay for what the business needs.

How do revisions work?

Review points and the number of revision rounds are written into the plan before work starts, so both sides know what to expect.

Who owns the final work?

Ownership and where you can use the work are written into the plan. If you need something specific, such as using footage in paid ads for a set period, tell us in the first meeting.

Can we meet online?

Yes. Tell us what suits you when you get in touch.
