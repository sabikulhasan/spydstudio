Frequently
asked questions

Anything else? Ask us directly.

How does a project start?

With a conversation. You tell us about the business and what you need, by the form, WhatsApp or email. We then set up a call to discuss it properly before anything is quoted or made.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope, so we quote after the first meeting. The quote names every deliverable, the format and the date, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

How long does it take?

We agree the timeline in the meeting and write it into the plan. We give a date for every deliverable, and we tell you early if anything changes.

What do you need from us?

Access to the product or service, any brand files you have, and someone who can answer questions and approve the plan. We list anything else in the plan.

Can we combine services?

Yes. A launch might need a video ad, UGC clips, a landing page and the campaign to run them. We plan them together so they share one message.
