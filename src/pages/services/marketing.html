What a monthly report looks like.

When Marketing is in scope, you get a short report each month. It leads with what the money did, shows what didn’t work as plainly as what did, and ends with what changes next. This is a sample with example figures.

What worked

The “question” hook brought most of the enquiries at the lowest cost.

What didn’t

Click-through fell on the second week’s video. Fewer people tapped, so we paused it.

Next month

Move its budget to the question hook and test two new openings. Budget stays the same.

Context, not headlines: reach, likes and new followers are included in the appendix. We don’t lead with them because they don’t show whether the ads paid off.
