How a project works.

Every project follows the same four steps: brief, brand study, written plan, then production and delivery. You know what we are making, and why, before production starts.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We discuss the product, audience, goal and existing material, by WhatsApp, email or an online call.

    You receive: a clear record of the requirement.

    From you: a short description and a time for the call.

  2. 02

    Brand study

    We review the offer, the audience and relevant competitors. This is where the idea comes from.

    You receive: the proposed message and approach.

    From you: access to the product and any brand files you have.

  3. 03

    Written plan

    We agree scope, formats, timeline and review points before production. Nothing starts until you approve it.

    You receive: a written plan to approve.

    From you: approval of the written plan.

  4. 04

    Production & delivery

    We create the agreed work, review it with you at the points in the plan, and deliver the agreed files. If anything changes, we tell you early. Production and paid-media management are separate scopes.

    You receive: the final files in the agreed formats.

    From you: feedback at each review point.

Questions about the process

Do you have fixed packages?

No. We scope every project with you in a meeting, so you only pay for what the business needs.

How do revisions work?

Review points and the number of revision rounds are written into the plan before work starts, so both sides know what to expect.

Who owns the final work?

Ownership and where you can use the work are written into the plan. If you need something specific, such as using footage in paid ads for a set period, tell us in the first meeting.

Can we meet online?

Yes. Tell us what suits you when you get in touch.

Tell us what you need to make.

Share the business, the audience and the goal. We’ll discuss the approach and scope with you.
